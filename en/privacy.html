Privacy Policy

This is a translation of the Japanese original. If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

The Pinzu operator (hereinafter the "Company") establishes this Privacy Policy (hereinafter this "Policy") as set out below regarding the handling of users' personal information in the service "Pinzu" (Japanese name: 地図コレ) provided by the Company (hereinafter the "Service").

Article 1 (Information We Collect)

In providing the Service, the Company collects the following information.

  • Information entered by users at registration (username, email address, profile image, etc.)
  • Content posted by users through the Service (posted images, text, location information, etc.)
  • Information about the use of the Service (access logs, cookie information, device information, etc.)
  • Information obtained through integration with external services such as Apple ID and Google accounts
  • Device tokens for sending push notifications
  • Advertising identifiers for delivering and measuring advertisements

Article 2 (Purposes of Use)

The Company uses the collected information for the following purposes.

  • To provide, maintain, protect, and improve the Service
  • To accept registrations for the Service, verify identity, authenticate users, record user settings, and the like
  • To respond to inquiries from users
  • To analyze the use of the Service and use the results to improve the Service
  • To respond to acts that violate the terms, policies, and the like relating to the Service (hereinafter the "Terms, etc.")
  • To contact users as necessary, such as with important notices regarding the Service
  • For purposes incidental to the above purposes of use

Article 3 (Provision to Third Parties)

The Company will not provide users' personal information to third parties except in any of the following cases.

  • When the user has given consent
  • When required by laws and regulations
  • When it is necessary for the protection of a person's life, body, or property and it is difficult to obtain the user's consent
  • When it is especially necessary for improving public health or promoting the sound upbringing of children and it is difficult to obtain the user's consent
  • When it is necessary to cooperate with a national government agency, a local government, or a party entrusted by either of them in performing affairs prescribed by laws and regulations, and obtaining the user's consent is likely to impede the performance of those affairs

Article 4 (Disclosure, Correction, and Deletion of Personal Information)

Users may make the following requests in accordance with the procedures specified by the Company.

  • A request for notification of the purposes of use of personal information
  • A request for disclosure of personal information
  • A request for correction, addition, or deletion of personal information
  • A request to stop the use of personal information

When the Company receives any of the above requests from a user, it will respond without delay.

Article 5 (Use of External Services)

The Service uses the following external services. For the handling of personal information in these services, please refer to the privacy policy of each service.

  • Firebase (Google LLC) - Authentication, database, storage
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) - Social login
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) - Social login
  • Google Mobile Ads (Google LLC) - Ad delivery
  • MusicKit (Apple Inc.) - Retrieval of music information

Article 6 (Use of Cookies, etc.)

The Service may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user convenience and the Service. Users can refuse to accept cookies through their browser settings; in that case, however, some functions of the Service may become unavailable.

Article 7 (Access Analysis Tools)

The Service may use access analysis tools to improve the Service. Access analysis tools use cookies to collect information about user behavior, but this information does not include anything that identifies an individual.

Article 8 (Use of Location Information)

With the user's consent, the Service obtains location information in order to display candidate places for placing a pin. Location information is used only to record the position of pins and to search for nearby places.

The use of location information can be turned off at any time from iOS "Settings" > "Privacy & Security" > "Location Services."

Article 9 (Ad Delivery)

The Service delivers advertisements using Google AdMob provided by Google LLC. AdMob may use information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) to deliver and measure advertisements.

The Company does not collect information for the purpose of tracking users. If you do not want personalized advertising, you can change this from iOS "Settings" > "Privacy & Security" > "Tracking."

For the handling of data in AdMob, please refer to Google's advertising policies.

Article 10 (Push Notifications)

The Service may send push notifications to inform you of follows, likes, comments, and the like. Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging are used for sending, and for that purpose the device's notification token is stored.

Notifications can be turned off at any time from iOS "Settings" > "Notifications." Even if notifications are turned off, you can continue to use the other functions of the Service.

Article 11 (Data Retention Period and Account Deletion)

Pins, comments, and maps posted by users are stored until the user deletes them or deletes their account.

Users can delete their own account at any time from "Settings" > "Delete Account" in the app. When an account is deleted, data including posts, comments, and follow relationships will be deleted and cannot be restored.

Article 12 (Information Related to Reports and Blocks)

When a user blocks another user, the Company stores the information necessary to maintain that state. When the Company receives a report of inappropriate content, it stores the content of the report as a record of its response.

Article 13 (Children's Privacy)

The Service is not directed at persons under the age of 13. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from persons under the age of 13. If it is discovered that information from a person under the age of 13 has been collected, the Company will delete it promptly.

Article 14 (Changes to this Privacy Policy)

The Company may change this Policy as necessary. The revised Privacy Policy takes effect when it is posted on the Service.

Article 15 (Contact)

For inquiries regarding this Policy, please contact us through the contact form in the Service.
